How to get YouTube cookies in 1 minute (Free & Safe):

  1. Install the free Chrome/Edge extension: "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY".
  2. Open youtube.com in a new tab.
  3. Click the extension icon, choose "Export" (or copy the text), then paste it into the box above and click Save Cookies!
  4. Alternatively: You can also add an Environment Variable in Render: YOUTUBE_COOKIES = (your cookies text).
